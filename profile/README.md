# Pixel Journey 🛸

**Welcome to Pixel Journey**

Pixel Journey is a Web3 educational and creative ecosystem focused on pixel art, NFTs, games, and meaningful digital experiences. We primarily build on WAX while designing for future multi-chain and cross-chain expansion.

We guide communities through engaging journeys that combine art, education, staking, marketplaces, gaming, and on-chain creation. Our mission is to help people explore, learn, create, and enjoy the opportunities that Web3 has to offer — **Px by Px, Journey by Journey**.

We believe Web3 should feel joyful, accessible, empowering, and fun. Everything we build aims to lower barriers while raising the bar for quality, education, and user experience.

## PxPortals

PxPortals are our user-facing experiences. Most currently run on WAX with plans to expand to additional chains over time.

### Current & Active PxPortals

- **PxWallet** — Secure self-custody wallet with strong focus on usability, security, and multi-account management.
- **PxStaking** — [staking.pixeljourney.xyz](https://staking.pixeljourney.xyz)  
  Stake assets and participate in ecosystem growth with sustainable incentives.
- **PxMarket** — [market.pixeljourney.xyz](https://market.pixeljourney.xyz)  
  Curated marketplace for discovering and trading Pixel Journey NFTs and assets.
- **PxLearn** — Educational portal and learning hub with guides, tutorials, and interactive content.
- **PxHot** — Community voting and engagement portal (Hot or Not style experiences).
- **PxSwap** — Token and asset swapping experience with focus on usability and fair mechanics.
- **PxLanding** — Main landing and entry experiences for the ecosystem.

### Planned & Future PxPortals

- **PxCreate** — Creator tools and studio for designing, minting, and managing pixel art and on-chain assets.
- **PxInsights** — Analytics, dashboards, and on-chain insights to help users and creators understand activity and opportunities.
- **PxPredict** — Future portal focused on prediction markets, forecasting, and community-driven insights (aspirational).

## Our Philosophy

- **Existing Public Primitives First** — We maximize established on-chain infrastructure rather than building unnecessary custom contracts.
- **Self-Custody & Security** — Strong emphasis on user-controlled keys and secure experiences.
- **Educational Value** — Everything we create should help people learn and grow.
- **High-Quality UI/UX** — Distinctive retro pixel aesthetic with premium execution.
- **Verifiable & Transparent** — On-chain, verifiable mechanics wherever possible.

These principles are documented in our [Standards Codex](https://github.com/pixel-journey/pixel-journey-standards).
